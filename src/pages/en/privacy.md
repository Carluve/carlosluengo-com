_This is a translation; the Spanish version prevails._

Site owner: Carlos Luengo Vera. Domain: carlosluengo.com. The site is published as static pages.

## What this site does not do

There is no contact form, newsletter or user area. There are no advertising pixels and no cross-site tracking. Personal data is not sold or passed on to third parties for commercial purposes.

Correspondence is handled via [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/carlosluengo), outside this site.

## Visit statistics

To know which pages get read, the site uses [Cloudflare Web Analytics](https://www.cloudflare.com/web-analytics/). A small script (`static.cloudflareinsights.com`) measures each visit in aggregate: page viewed, referring site, country, browser and device type, and load times. It uses no cookies or local storage, does not fingerprint the browser and does not follow anyone across sites. The data is only seen in aggregate, never per person. If your browser blocks the script, the site works just the same.

In addition, **only if you accept** in the cookie notice, the site loads Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited). It measures which articles get read and for how long, and sets the `_ga` and `_ga_*` cookies listed below. If you reject or don't choose, Google Analytics is not downloaded and nothing is sent to Google. Advertising signals are turned off. The legal basis is your consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR and art. 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI), and you can withdraw it at any time with this button:

<button type="button" data-consent-open class="px-4 py-2 rounded-md border border-gray-300 dark:border-gray-600 font-medium">Change my cookie preferences</button>

Google may process this data outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards described in its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Cookies and local storage

This site uses no advertising cookies. The only analytics cookies are Google Analytics ones, and they are only set if you accept them in the notice. This is what may be stored in your browser:

| Name      | Type              | Set by                                     | Purpose                                          | Lifetime           |
| --------- | ----------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------ |
| `__cf_bm` | Technical cookie  | Cloudflare                                 | Telling people from bots and protecting the site | 30 minutes         |
| `lang`    | Preference cookie | This site, only if you click ES/EN         | Remembering the language you chose               | 1 year             |
| `theme`   | `localStorage`    | This site, only if you change the theme    | Remembering light or dark mode                   | Until you clear it |
| `mode`    | `localStorage`    | This site, only if you press Human/Machine | Remembering the regular or Markdown view         | Until you clear it |
| `consent` | `localStorage`    | This site, when you accept or reject       | Remembering your analytics cookie choice         | Until you clear it |
| `_ga`     | Analytics cookie  | Google Analytics, only if you accept       | Telling visitors apart pseudonymously            | 2 years            |
| `_ga_*`   | Analytics cookie  | Google Analytics, only if you accept       | Keeping session state                            | 2 years            |

`__cf_bm`, `lang`, `theme`, `mode` and `consent` are technical cookies or preferences you asked for, exempt from consent under article 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI. `_ga` and `_ga_*` only exist if you give consent. You can delete any of them at any time from your browser settings.

## Hosting

The static site is published on Cloudflare Workers, and the images, videos and PDFs of the articles are served from Cloudflare R2 (media.carlosluengo.com). The provider may process technical connection data (for example, IP address or user agent) to deliver the site and protect the network. That processing is governed by [Cloudflare’s privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).
