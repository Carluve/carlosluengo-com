_This is a translation; the Spanish version prevails._

Site owner: Carlos Luengo Vera. Domain: carlosluengo.com. The site is published as static pages.

## What this site does not do

There is no contact form, newsletter or user area. No analytics tools or tracking pixels are installed. Personal data is not sold or passed on to third parties for commercial purposes.

Correspondence is handled via [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/carlosluengo), outside this site.

## Cookies and local storage

This site uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, which is why it shows no consent banner. It only stores in your browser what it needs to work and what you choose:

| Name      | Type              | Set by                                  | Purpose                                          | Lifetime           |
| --------- | ----------------- | --------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------ |
| `__cf_bm` | Technical cookie  | Cloudflare                              | Telling people from bots and protecting the site | 30 minutes         |
| `lang`    | Preference cookie | This site, only if you click ES/EN      | Remembering the language you chose               | 1 year             |
| `theme`   | `localStorage`    | This site, only if you change the theme | Remembering light or dark mode                   | Until you clear it |

These are technical cookies or preferences you asked for, exempt from consent under article 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI. None of them identifies you or is shared with third parties, and you can delete them at any time from your browser settings.

## Hosting

The static site is published on Cloudflare Workers, and the images, videos and PDFs of the articles are served from Cloudflare R2 (media.carlosluengo.com). The provider may process technical connection data (for example, IP address or user agent) to deliver the site and protect the network. That processing is governed by [Cloudflare’s privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

## External links

LinkedIn, GitHub, Google Scholar, arXiv and the other linked sites apply their own terms. Following a link takes you away from this site.

## Exercising your rights

This site does not keep a file of personal data about its visitors. For any question about this page, the channel is the LinkedIn profile given above.
